# Lodestar

A helper for AFK Journey on PC (Steam).

**Download.** Get `Lodestar-<version>.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/ImCrowns/lodestar-releases/releases/latest). Unzip it and read "Read me first.txt".

An installed copy updates itself. When a new version is out, an Update button shows at the top of its window.

Lodestar is not made or approved by the makers of AFK Journey. Automation may be against the game's terms of service. Use it at your own risk.
